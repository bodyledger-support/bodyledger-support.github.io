Body Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Body Ledger app ("Body Ledger," "we," "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Body Ledger is made by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS]. Questions: bodyledger.support@gmail.com.

The short version:

  • Most of what you log stays on your iPhone. Food, workouts, check-ins, vitals, measurements, progress photos and cycle data are stored on your device and, where you allow it, in Apple Health.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Only the Friends feature sends data to our servers, and only if you create an account. It sends your account details, the workouts you choose to share, and your likes and comments.
  • You can export or delete everything from inside the app.

2. Apple Health

With your permission, Body Ledger reads from and writes to Apple Health. You choose exactly which types in the Health permission screen, and you can change this anytime in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Body Ledger.

  • We read: sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed, active energy, workouts, weight, body fat, waist size, blood pressure, blood sugar, blood oxygen, water, height, date of birth, biological sex, and (only if you turn on cycle tracking) menstrual flow.
  • We write: the food, water, weigh-ins, waist measurements, blood pressure, blood sugar, workouts and (only if you turn on cycle tracking) period days you log in Body Ledger.

Health data is used only to run the app's features for you. We never use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, never sell it, never share it with data brokers, and never send it to our servers. The only exception: if you choose to share a workout with friends, its summary (described in section 3) is sent, and you can leave heart rate out.

4. Food database searches

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product's nutrition facts. Meals you describe and nutrition labels you photograph are read on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence and Apple's text recognition, and are not sent to us or anyone else. We don't send your name, account or any health information with these requests. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no selling or renting of data.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • No location tracking.
  • No use of your data to train AI models.
  • No sharing with insurers, employers or data brokers.

6. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: targets, trends, suggestions, sharing with friends.
  • To keep the Friends feature safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks the Terms of Use, and acting on blocks.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-in and password reset.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Suggestions such as "your usual breakfast" and estimates such as measured maintenance calories are worked out on your device from your own logs.

7. How long we keep it

  • Device data: until you delete it, use Profile › Delete all my data, or delete the app.
  • Friends data: until you delete it or your account. Profile › Friends and sharing › Delete account removes your account, profile, photo, shared workouts, follows, likes and comments from our servers right away. Encrypted backups held by our provider may keep copies for up to 7 days before they're overwritten.
  • Reports may be kept for up to [e.g. 1 year] to handle repeat abuse, without the deleted account's content.

8. Your choices and rights

From inside the app you can:

  • See and export your data: Profile › Export my data saves a copy of everything you've logged. Progress photos aren't in the file; they stay in the app, where you can view or delete them.
  • Correct anything by editing it.
  • Delete everything on the device (Profile › Delete all my data) or your Friends account (Profile › Friends and sharing › Delete account).
  • Withdraw Apple Health access in iPhone Settings.
  • Stop sharing any workout, switch off auto-share, or make your account private.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Texas, Washington, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, to opt out of "sale" or targeted advertising (we do neither), and to appeal our decision on a request. Email bodyledger.support@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request." We'll confirm your identity using your account email, respond within the time the law requires (usually 30–45 days), and won't treat you differently for using your rights. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. If the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

Consumer health data: see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for the details required by Washington, Nevada and similar state laws.

9. Children

Body Ledger isn't meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK), and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created a Friends account, email bodyledger.support@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database rules that stop people seeing workouts they're not allowed to see. Device data is protected by iOS encryption. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires, including under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.
