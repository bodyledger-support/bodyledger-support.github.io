Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email bodyledger.support@gmail.com. In the app you can also go to Profile › Contact support, which fills in your app version for you. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Food, workouts, body measurements and check-ins never leave it unless you choose to share a workout with friends. Health data you allow is read from and saved to Apple Health.

How do I export or delete my data?

  • Export: Profile › Export my data saves a copy you can keep.
  • Delete everything on this iPhone: Profile › Delete all my data.
  • Delete your Friends account and everything on our servers: Profile › Friends and sharing › Delete account. It takes effect right away.

Describe or meal photos don't work

Describing meals uses Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. You can always scan a barcode, photograph a nutrition label, or type the numbers in.

Steps, sleep or heart rate are missing

Open the Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Body Ledger and turn on the categories you want. Readiness needs a few nights of sleep and heart-rate data from an Apple Watch.

I'm not getting reminders

Check Settings › Notifications › Body Ledger. Reminders are skipped on purpose when you've already logged that meal, and pause after a few days away.

Is this medical advice?

No. Body Ledger gives estimates and general guidance. Talk to a doctor before big changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you have a health condition. In the app, see Profile › Health information.
