Body Ledger Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy covers "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's SB 370 and similar state laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy. Contact: bodyledger.support@gmail.com.

What consumer health data we collect, and where it lives

On your iPhone only. We never receive this data.

  • Food, nutrition and water logs
  • Weight, body fat and body measurements
  • Sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps and activity
  • Blood pressure, blood sugar, blood oxygen and temperature
  • Menstrual cycle and period days (only if you turn on cycle tracking)
  • Progress photos
  • Workouts and exercise history

On our servers, only if you create a Friends account and choose to share:

  • Summaries of workouts you share: exercises, sets, reps, weights, duration and calories. You can also include average and maximum heart rate and a heart-rate chart, or leave heart rate out.

Sources

  • What you enter in the app.
  • Apple Health, with your permission. You choose each data type and can withdraw access at any time.

Why we use it

Only to provide the features you ask for:

  • calculating targets, trends and suggestions on your device;
  • showing workouts you choose to share to the friends you choose.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

  • Your followers: only the workouts you choose to share.
  • Supabase, our hosting provider (a processor), to store shared workouts on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, such as a valid court order. We'll tell you unless the law forbids it.

We don't share consumer health data with any other third party or affiliate. We never collect precise location.

Consent

We collect consumer health data only with your consent: the Apple Health permission screen, and your choice to share each workout (or to turn on auto-share). You can withdraw consent at any time:

  • Turn off Apple Health access in iPhone Settings.
  • Turn off auto-share, or stop sharing a workout.
  • Delete your data (see below).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • get a list of every third party and affiliate it was shared with;
  • access a copy of it (Profile › Export my data);
  • delete it: Profile › Delete all my data for device data, and Profile › Friends and sharing › Delete account for server data. Deletion on our servers takes effect right away, and backups are cleared within 7 days.

To make a request, email bodyledger.support@gmail.com. We'll respond within 45 days, which can be extended once by another 45 days if needed. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. We'll answer the appeal within 45 days. If the appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General (or your own state's attorney general).

Changes

We'll post any update here with a new effective date, and ask for your consent again before using consumer health data in a new way.
